# 📜 Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We as members, contributors, and leaders of the **Institute of Molecular Biology (IMB)** pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free and scientifically rigorous experience for everyone.

## Our Standards
* Demonstrating empathy, scientific integrity, and constructive feedback.
* Fostering inclusive academic collaboration.
* Respecting diverse viewpoints and research methodologies.
